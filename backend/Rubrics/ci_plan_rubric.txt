================================================================================
SECTION 5: CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT (CI) PLAN
================================================================================

Criterion 5.1: Prior CI Considered (If Not a New Program / LO)
- Description: Evaluation of past improvement initiatives.
- Scoring:
  * 0.0 points: Not a new program or LO, but no prior CI effort is discussed.
  * 0.5 points: Prior CI plan is discussed, but its effectiveness was not determined using evidence (relies merely on opinion, supposition, or informal review).
  * 1.0 points: Explicitly a new program or new LO; OR prior CI relevant to this LO is discussed and its effectiveness is determined and substantiated with evidence.

Criterion 5.2: Evidence-Based Fashion of Prior CI
- Description: Verification that prior CI evaluation used tangible outcome metrics.
- Scoring:
  * 0.0 points: No data or evidence provided (opinion/anecdotal only).
  * 0.5 points: Claims made, but links connecting prior CI efforts to LO achievement are weak or unclear.
  * 1.0 points: Specific, artifact-based student achievement evidence presented demonstrating the impact of the prior plan.

Criterion 5.3: Current CI - Consideration / No Changes Proposed
- Description: Deliberate determination that current standards require no revision.
- Scoring:
  * 0.0 points: Not checked or unexplained absence of proposal.
  * 1.0 points: Explicitly checked/justified that, after review, no curricular changes are needed (yielding 1 point; active change plans proceed below).

Criterion 5.4: Specific Curricular or Instructional Change Proposed
- Description: Concrete educational modifications addressing identified weaknesses.
- Scoring:
  * 0.0 points: No changes proposed, or proposals are strictly non-curricular.
  * 0.5 points: Curricular changes are vague, misaligned with the identified deficit, or focused on non-instructional criteria (e.g., altering admission requirements).
  * 1.0 points: Specific curricular (course/exam requirements) or instructional changes proposed, with a clear explanation of how they resolve the identified deficit.

Criterion 5.5: Administrative Implementation Process Described
- Description: Execution framework, personnel roles, and deadlines.
- Scoring:
  * 0.0 points: No implementation plan or administrative process described.
  * 0.5 points: Deficient in structure or timeline (e.g., no specific responsible party assigned, or missing target deadlines).
  * 1.0 points: Explicit administrative oversight and responsible party identified (curriculum committee, named faculty member, department chair) AND an implementation timeline is clearly provided.
