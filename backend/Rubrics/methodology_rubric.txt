================================================================================
SECTION 3: METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Criterion 3.1: Artifact-Based Assessment
- Description: Verification that direct student artifacts were evaluated.
- Scoring:
  * 0.0 points: No evidence student artifacts were used.
  * 1.0 points: Clear evidence that student artifacts were evaluated.

Criterion 3.2: Clear Rubric
- Description: Use and clarity of the grading rubric applied to student artifacts.
- Scoring:
  * 0.0 points: No evidence of a rubric.
  * 1.0 points: Rubric provided (embedded or as an active link) with clear descriptions of standards and how they apply to the selected artifact.

Criterion 3.3: Concordance with Learning Outcome (LO) Articulated
- Description: Alignment between student artifacts and the learning outcome.
- Scoring:
  * 0.0 points: No concordance of LO to rubric described.
  * 1.0 points: Clear, explicit statement explaining how the artifact directly aligns with the LO being assessed.

Criterion 3.4: Description of Categorization {D, M, P} or Equivalent
- Description: Clear metric categories (e.g., Developing, Meeting, Producing / Exceeding).
- Scoring:
  * 0.0 points: No description of {D, M, P} or performance categories.
  * 1.0 points: Definite algorithmic process used to assign {D, M, P} categories; OR an equivalent alternative classification with at least 3 distinct levels and clear descriptions of what constitutes meeting or exceeding expectations.
